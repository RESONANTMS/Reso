The Process

Four phases.
One outcome.

01
Weeks 1–2

Revenue Infrastructure Audit

A structured, two-week deep dive into your entire revenue operation. We examine every system, every channel, every data source — not to find fault, but to find leverage. Most businesses have significant revenue potential already present in their existing assets. We find it.

Marketing stack & channel performance review
Sales process and pipeline architecture audit
MarTech tools and integration mapping
Data flow and attribution analysis
Conversion system review (top to bottom)
ICP clarity and positioning assessment
Competitor intelligence and market positioning
AI and automation capability gap analysis
Deliverable: Revenue Infrastructure Report
02
Week 3

Bottleneck Identification

We synthesise the audit into a clear, prioritised picture of exactly what's limiting your revenue and by how much. We quantify the gap, rank the opportunities by impact, and align on a strategy before any implementation begins.

Revenue leak identification and quantification
Channel performance benchmarking
Technology gap analysis and prioritisation
Quick wins vs. long-term infrastructure plays
90-day growth roadmap with KPI targets
Projected revenue impact per initiative
Deliverable: Bottleneck & Opportunity Report
03
Weeks 4–10

Implementation & Testing

We build. Prioritised by impact, executed with precision. Every initiative is tracked against a defined KPI from day one — there is no "launch and hope" in our process. Systems are tested, iterated and confirmed before we move to the next phase.

Marketing system builds and channel activations
AI automation workflows and agent deployment
Funnel redesigns and CRO implementation
MarTech integration and data centralisation
Brand positioning and content system launch
Sales pipeline architecture and CRM configuration
Weekly performance reviews and iteration
Real-time KPI dashboard deployment
Deliverable: Live Systems Report
04
Ongoing

KPI Tracking & Standardisation

What works becomes permanent. Successful strategies are documented as standard operating procedures — ensuring growth is repeatable and doesn't depend on individual effort or our continued involvement. This is the compounding value of systems over campaigns.

Revenue KPI tracking dashboard (real-time)
Monthly performance review and optimisation
Standard Operating Procedure documentation
Team training and handoff protocols
Quarterly strategy recalibration
Continuous AI model refinement
Deliverable: KPI Dashboard & SOP Library

Expectations

We operate with
radical transparency.

Communication
Weekly performance calls

Every week you will receive a structured performance review — what moved, what didn't, and what we're doing about it. No surprises, no spin.

Reporting
Real-time KPI dashboard

You have access to a live revenue dashboard at all times. Every metric we track is visible to you, in real time, with full attribution.

Accountability
KPIs, not deliverables

We are accountable to revenue outcomes, not activity outputs. We track against KPIs we agreed at the start — and if a strategy isn't working, we change it fast.

Ownership
Everything belongs to you

Every system, every SOP, every workflow we build belongs entirely to your business. If we ever part ways, your growth infrastructure remains intact.
