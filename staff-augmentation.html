The Process

From requirement to
fully onboarded in 72 hours.

01

Requirement Scoping

A 30-minute briefing call to understand the role, responsibilities, required skill set, timezone overlap needs and your existing team workflow.

02

Candidate Matching

We match against our pre-vetted talent pool — specialists who have been assessed on technical capability, communication and deliverable quality.

03

Introduction Call

You speak directly with 1–3 matched candidates. Assess culture fit, communication style and approach before making a decision.

04

Onboarding

Your chosen specialist is integrated into your tools, communication channels and workflows. We facilitate the setup and handle initial coordination.

05

Ongoing Management

Regular performance reviews, structured feedback loops and the flexibility to scale hours up, down or add additional team members as your needs evolve.
