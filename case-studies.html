Unified paid media strategy with cross-location attribution

Lead scoring model segmented by vehicle category

Automated follow-up sequences by segment & intent

Live revenue dashboard visible to all stakeholders

Consolidated agency relationship — one point of contact

Professional certification provider · 28 employees · 4-month engagement

Summit was almost entirely referral-dependent with no structured digital presence, no nurture system and no ability to predict revenue month-to-month. Enrollment cycles were long, erratic and deeply dependent on two salespeople. Leadership wanted to scale without proportionally scaling headcount — but had no infrastructure to support that goal.

Key Pain Points

95%+ revenue from word-of-mouth referrals

No email nurture — just a monthly newsletter

Enrollment cycles 3–6 months, no predictability

2 salespeople doing all qualification manually

Brand positioning overhaul — clear authority messaging

Full SWEPS rollout: SEO, LinkedIn, AEO content strategy

AI-powered email automation across 5 enrollment segments

Webinar funnel — top-of-funnel to enrollment pipeline

CRM implementation with predictive lead scoring

Monthly enrollment forecast model and reporting

Lead qualification chatbot to pre-screen applicants

D2C E-commerce · Vancouver, Canada

Cascade D2C Brands

Direct-to-consumer outdoor lifestyle brand · 55 employees · 5-month engagement

The Challenge

Rising customer acquisition costs driven by platform CPMs increasing 60% YoY, poor customer retention (average LTV of just 1.4 purchases), no lifecycle marketing to speak of and a Klaviyo account that was essentially sending one monthly newsletter. Revenue was growing but margins were being destroyed by acquisition spend. The business was scaling without becoming more profitable.

Key Pain Points

CAC up 60% YoY due to rising CPMs

Average LTV of only 1.4 purchases per customer

Klaviyo account — one monthly newsletter, nothing else

No retention strategy — pure acquisition focus

Revenue growing but margin shrinking

Our Approach

Full email lifecycle automation across 8 behavioural segments

SMS nurture integration for high-intent signals

CRO overhaul of product pages and cart/checkout flow

Customer data platform (CDP) implementation

Loyalty & repurchase incentive system design

Retention-focused paid strategy — budget shifted to re-engagement

Predictive LTV modelling by customer cohort
