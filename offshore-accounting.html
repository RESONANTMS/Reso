Onboarding Process

From handover to fully operational
in 48 hours.

01

Discovery & Access

A structured onboarding call to understand your current accounting setup, chart of accounts, payroll schedule and reporting requirements. Secure access is granted to your existing platform.

02

Clean-Up (if required)

We assess the current state of your books. Where there are historical discrepancies, misclassifications or backlog, we handle clean-up before taking over ongoing work.

03

Handover & Live Operation

Your dedicated accounting specialist takes ownership of the agreed scope. Weekly/monthly deliverables are confirmed. A shared reporting schedule is established from day one.

04

Review & Scale

Monthly review calls with your account manager. Add-on services (CFO advisory, additional entities, payroll jurisdictions) can be introduced without additional onboarding friction.
