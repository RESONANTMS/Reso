Delivery Governance

Structure and accountability,
built into every engagement.

Stage 1

Preparation

Assigned finance professional completes all accounting work against documented internal procedures and the agreed reporting calendar.

Stage 2

Engagement Lead Review

Your Engagement Lead reviews every deliverable for completeness, accuracy, and consistency before it progresses further.

Stage 3

Exception Review

Where required, a senior-level exception review is conducted to address variances, anomalies, or complex reporting considerations.

Stage 4

Submission to Client

Only after all review stages are complete is the deliverable submitted to your team — with full documentation and a concise commentary where applicable.
